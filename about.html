About Techniqus

Making connectivity smarter, faster, and more accessible.

Techniqus Infosec Pvt Ltd is a trusted IT accessories & peripherals provider and system integrator, serving large corporates and enterprise clients. We combine innovation with strong vendor partnerships to deliver reliable products and services across industries.

Our Story

Founded in 2018

Techniqus is dedicated to creating smart connectivity solutions that simplify everyday life. Our portfolio includes hubs, docks, adapters, SSDs, and innovative gadgets designed to keep people connected — whether at work, on the move, or at play.


We are a new-age brand built on a strong foundation of data storage and peripheral expertise, now expanding into solutions that combine speed, reliability, and convenience. Every product is crafted with a focus on performance and durability, ensuring that professionals and everyday users can depend on Techniqus.

What We Believe

At Techniqus, we see technology as an enabler of better living. By collaborating with leading partners and embracing innovation, we deliver products that help you share, stream, and collaborate effortlessly.

Our Vision

Our vision is simple: to make connectivity smarter, faster, and more accessible — bringing the digital world closer to you, every day.
